# 💎 Brand Identity Kit (`brandkit/`)

This directory is the **single source of truth for your brand assets**. Any logo, color palette, custom font, watermarks, recurring avatar cutouts, or audio signatures belong here so that all video pipelines can access them reliably.

---

## 📂 What Goes in Here?

1. **Logos & Badges:**
   - Vector files (`.svg`) and high-resolution transparent PNGs (`.png` at 300 DPI).
   - Variations: Dark mode logo, light mode logo, monochrome, icon-only / favicon, watermark / bug.
2. **Typography & Fonts:**
   - Custom font files (`.ttf`, `.otf`, `.woff2`) for titles, body, and caption overlays.
3. **Color Palette:**
   - `colors.json` or markdown documentation listing brand HEX, RGB, and HSL codes.
4. **Audio Branding:**
   - Audio signature / stinger (`.wav`, `.mp3`) for video intros and outros.
   - Licensed background music tracks and recurring audio themes.
5. **Overlays & Reusable Motion Graphics:**
   - Verified checkmarks, subscribe buttons, like/share animations, "Link in Bio" badges, social media handle overlays.
6. **Avatar & Creator Visual Bank:**
   - Transparent cutout PNGs of your AI avatar or creator poses (pointing, thinking, shocked, smiling).
   - Consistent avatar reference seeds or LoRA models.

## 🔤 Typography Standards

* **Heading / Kinetic Font:** e.g., *Montserrat Black*, *The Bold Font*, or *Futura Extra Bold*
* **Body / Subtitle Font:** e.g., *Inter*, *SF Pro Display*, or *Poppins Bold*
* **Code / Monospace:** e.g., *JetBrains Mono* or *Fira Code*
