# 🏆 Approved Videos & Hall of Fame (`approved videos/`)

This directory houses your **gold standard**—every finished edit that has passed your quality review and is officially approved for publishing or delivery.

---

## 📂 What Goes in Here?

1. **Master High-Res Renders:** Pristine, finalized exports (`.mp4`, `.mov`) in native resolution (4K or 1080p, 60fps / 30fps).
2. **Platform Ready Masters:**
   - **Vertical (9:16):** Finished Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and TikToks.
   - **Horizontal (16:9):** Finished YouTube main videos, web presentations, and tutorials.
   - **Square / Portrait (1:1 / 4:5):** LinkedIn / Twitter / Instagram feed deliverables.
3. **Approved Production Packages:** Archive copies of final scripts, prompt recipes, or project configurations that yielded winning videos.
4. **Performance Notes:** Brief post-mortem notes on views, retention rates, and audience reactions.

---

## 🏷️ Naming Convention

Keep files organized chronologically with standard naming:
```
YYYY-MM-DD_<project-slug>_<aspect-ratio>_FINAL_APPROVED.mp4
```
**Examples:**
* `2026-11-12_ai-avatar-tutorial_9x16_FINAL_APPROVED.mp4`
* `2026-11-15_hyperframes-case-study_16x9_FINAL_APPROVED.mp4`

---

## ✅ Quality Checklist (Before Moving a Video Here)

Before moving any video from `exports/` into `approved videos/`, ensure:
- [ ] **Audio Balance:** Voiceover is crisp, clear, normalized (-14 to -16 LUFS); BGM sits cleanly behind speech (-18dB to -24dB).
- [ ] **Subtitles / Captions:** Zero typos, perfectly synced, placed within mobile UI safe zones (not blocked by like buttons or captions bar).
- [ ] **Retention Hooks:** Immediate visual and audio stimulus in seconds 0–3.
- [ ] **Color & Contrast:** Consistent lighting, vibrant colors, and sharp text readability on dark and light backgrounds.
- [ ] **Call to Action (CTA):** Clear and compelling end action.
