# 🎬 Style & Inspiration References (`references/`)

This directory is your **visual benchmark and inspiration vault**. Store videos, clips, and visual breakdowns whose editing pace, style, color palette, or storytelling techniques you admire and want to emulate.

---

## 📂 What Goes in Here?

1. **Benchmark Video Clips:** Downloaded reels, shorts, or long-form video clips (`.mp4`, `.mov`, `.webm`) whose editing quality you want to replicate.
2. **Curated Link Lists (`links.md`):** URLs to viral Instagram Reels, TikToks, YouTube videos, and creator profiles with brief notes on what makes them great.
3. **Visual Moodboards & Screenshots:** Screen captures (`.png`, `.jpg`) of standout motion graphics, color grades, text animation styles, and thumbnails.
4. **Pacing & Hook Breakdowns:** Timestamped notes detailing hook formulas, average cut frequency, and sound design choices.

---

## 🗂️ Suggested Organization (Optional Subfolders)

```
references/
├── hooks/                # High-retention first 3-second visual & auditory hooks
├── creators/             # Subfolders per creator (e.g., /vox-style, /hormozi, /iman)
├── motion_graphics/      # Kinetic typography, lower thirds, UI animation examples
├── sound_design/         # Audio clips with punchy SFX, risers, and music transitions
└── links.md              # Running list of inspiration links and notes
```

---

## ⚡ Deconstruction Checklist (When Analyzing a Reference)

When adding a video here for the AI to study or reference, break down:
* **The Hook (0–3s):** What text appeared? What SFX played? Was there a camera punch-in?
* **Pacing & Cuts:** How many seconds does each shot last? (Standard fast-paced: 1.0–2.0s per shot).
* **Sound FX:** Where are whooshes, pops, bass drops, or keyboard taps placed?
* **Visual Metaphors:** How did they turn an abstract concept into an engaging graphic or micro-moment?
