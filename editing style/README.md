# 🎨 Editing Style Guide & System Directives (`editing style/`)

This directory is your **editorial rulebook and creative DNA**. It defines how your videos look, feel, sound, transition, and flow. Both AI generation scripts (HyperFrames, Remotion, FFmpeg, Python) and human editors use this guide to ensure 100% stylistic consistency.

---

## 📂 What Goes in Here?

1. **`style_guide.md`:** Comprehensive document outlining pacing, motion rules, font pairings, and caption styles.
2. **Color Grading & LUTs:** Cube LUT files (`.cube`), Lightroom / Premiere presets, and color treatment specifications (e.g., contrast curves, saturation levels, teal/orange or dark cinematic tones).
3. **Motion & Animation Presets:** Transition timings, zoom levels (e.g., 110% punch-in on key statements), easing curves (`cubic-bezier`), and motion graphic templates.
4. **Caption & Kinetic Text Rules:**
   - Word display limit (1–3 words at a time vs. full sentences).
   - Word highlighting colors (e.g., yellow `#FFE600` or cyan `#00F0FF` for stressed keywords).
   - Pop / bounce animation curves.
5. **Sound Design Blueprint:** Rules for placing whooshes, UI pops, risers, braams, and background music ducking levels.
6. **AI Prompt Recipes:** Master visual prompts for Midjourney, Flux, Stable Diffusion, Runway Gen-3, or Luma Dream Machine to maintain consistent character lighting, lens choice, and aspect ratios.

---

## ⚡ Studio Rhythm & Cadence Rules

* **Hook Window (0–3 Seconds):** Visual shift, zoom, or motion element every **0.8 to 1.2 seconds**. Fast visual stimulation prevents drop-off.
* **Body Section (3s to CTA):** Dynamic visual change (B-roll, screen capture, graphic overlay, or camera punch-in) every **1.5 to 2.5 seconds**. Avoid static talking head shots lasting more than 3 seconds.
* **Sound FX Cadence:** Pair every text punch-in, graphic pop, or chapter shift with a subtle sound effect.
